The fee buys real stocks.

  1. 01HoldKeep at least 1,000 $FOLIO through a full UTC day. No staking, no locking.1,000 min.
  2. 02Fee buys stocksEvery buy and sell pays 3% to the FolioEngine contract. About once an hour it swaps the ETH into eight stock tokens on Uniswap: 95% for holders, 5% for running costs.95 / 5
  3. 03Daily splitAt 00:00 UTC the day's stocks are split by the lowest balance each wallet held. The list is public for 6 hours first.00:00 UTC
  4. 04ClaimOne transaction sends all eight stocks to your wallet. Days add up. No deadline.Any time

    06 · Trust

    Where the trust sits, in plain words.

    Enforced by the contracts

    • No withdraw function for the stocks. The only way out is a holder's claim.
    • Swaps only through official Uniswap pools, checked on chain.
    • Running-costs cut is 5%; the contract refuses anything above 10%.
    • Each daily list waits before claims open: at least 1 hour by code, set to 6 hours.

    What you still trust us for

    • Our server works out each day's split. Every list is published so anyone can check it during the wait.
    • The stock tokens are Robinhood's. Robinhood can pause one or block an address; the other stocks still pay out.
    • Tested against a copy of the live chain. No outside audit yet.
