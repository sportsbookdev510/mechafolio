What the owner can and cannot do

  • Cannot withdraw fees, WETH or stocks to any wallet.
  • Cannot route swaps through anything but official Uniswap v3 pools (checked on chain).
  • Cannot raise the operations cut above 10%.
  • Cannot make a new root claimable without the public waiting period.
  • Can cancel a root during its waiting period if it is wrong.
  • Can repair a swap route, set the keeper and the operations wallet.
